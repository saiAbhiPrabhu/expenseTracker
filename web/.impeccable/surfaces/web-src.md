# Surface brief: whole front end (web/src)

Mode: Operate (demo steps); Home and Log in are short entry screens in the same system.
Audience: prospective end users judging whether to trust the product with a real statement.
Unresolved: exact hue tuned for contrast (teal-green); named action labels not adopted; no dark theme.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app becomes an instrument, not a document: one fixed control column (five step keys, then Back and the primary action) beside one flat working surface. Refuses the stacked column with an underlined step list and bordered box.
OWN-WORLD: Dieter Rams, pale glacial greys with near-black type, one bold teal-green signal used only on controls (primary action, current step key, focus). 4/8px grid, 2px corners, no shadows or gradients or icons, system sans in two weights, tabular numerals, flat dark bars on a light track.
STORY: The visitor sees the sequence and the one action at all times, reads each pipeline stage as a plain table, and leaves knowing where the money goes. Everything is labelled synthetic; disabled controls say why.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: slim top bar (wordmark left, three text links right); below it a 240px control column left (five numbered step keys, sample-data note, then Back + signal-colored primary key with its disabled reason underneath) and a wide surface right with the step title and one instruction line above the step content.
FORM: Rams (user-pinned, no roll), position 1 of 1, seed: none (unavailable).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
